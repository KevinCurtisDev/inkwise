# Privacy policy — Inkwise

**Last updated:** 25/11/2026

Inkwise is a Chrome extension for drawing, highlighting and writing on web pages and PDFs.

## Your annotations stay on your device
Everything you draw or write, your comments, the changes you try with Edit Mode, and the list of pages you've annotated are stored in your browser's local extension storage on your computer. They are never sent to us or to anyone else. We don't run a server, and the extension makes no network requests.

## Your preferences
Your tool settings (the colour and size you last used for each tool, your last tool, and the palm-rejection setting) and your export layout settings (which parts to include, and the author name you choose to show on exports, if any) are saved with Chrome's sync storage. If you've turned on Chrome sync, Chrome keeps them in your Google account so they follow you to your other computers. This is handled by Google under Google's privacy policy; we never see them. No annotations, comments or page addresses are stored there. Leave the author name empty if you don't want it synced.

## What the extension can access
- It runs on a tab only after you click its icon or press its shortcut, and only on that tab.
- On that tab it reads the page's layout, so your marks stay in place when you scroll or the page changes. It doesn't send the page's content anywhere. It saves, on your device only: the page's address and title (so you can find your work in the Library); for a mark or comment that follows a page element, or an Edit Mode change, a short piece of that element's text (at most 64 characters, plus up to 64 on either side) so it can find the element again; and, for Edit Mode text changes, the original text you replaced, so the change can be undone.
- Edit Mode changes how the page looks in your own browser tab only. The website itself isn't changed, and nobody else sees your changes.
- When you export an image or PDF, it takes screenshots of the tab: the visible part, or, for a full-page export, the whole page one screen at a time (it scrolls the page and briefly hides fixed headers and footers, then puts everything back). The file is saved to your computer or copied to your clipboard, and goes nowhere else. PDFs are made on your computer.
- PDFs you open in the PDF annotator are read on your computer and never uploaded.

## What we collect
Nothing. No account, no analytics, no tracking, no advertising, no selling or sharing of data.

## Your control
- Delete any page's annotations from the Library in the extension's Options, or use the Clear all button on the page. Delete comments one by one, and reset Edit Mode changes with Reset all.
- Back up all your annotations to a file you keep, and restore them later.
- Removing the extension deletes everything it stored on your device.

## Children
The extension collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

## Changes
If this policy changes, the new version will be published here with a new date. If a future version ever sends data off your device (for example, to process a payment), this policy will say so before that version is released.
