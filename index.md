# Privacy policy — Inkwise

**Last updated:** 16/11/2026

Inkwise is a Chrome extension for drawing, highlighting and writing on web pages and PDFs.

## Your annotations stay on your device
Everything you draw or write, and the list of pages you've annotated, is stored in your browser's local extension storage on your computer. It is never sent to us or to anyone else. We don't run a server, and the extension makes no network requests.

## Your preferences
Your tool settings (the colour and size you last used for each tool, your last tool, and the palm-rejection setting) are saved with Chrome's sync storage. If you've turned on Chrome sync, Chrome keeps them in your Google account so they follow you to your other computers. This is handled by Google under Google's privacy policy; we never see them. No annotations or page addresses are stored there.

## What the extension can access
- It runs on a tab only after you click its icon or press its shortcut, and only on that tab.
- On that tab it reads the page's layout, so your marks stay in place when you scroll or the page changes. It doesn't read, store or send the page's content, except for the page's address and title, which it saves with your annotations so you can find them in the Library.
- When you export an image, it takes a screenshot of the visible part of the tab. The image is saved to your computer or copied to your clipboard, and goes nowhere else.
- PDFs you open in the PDF annotator are read on your computer and never uploaded.

## What we collect
Nothing. No account, no analytics, no tracking, no advertising, no selling or sharing of data.

## Your control
- Delete any page's annotations from the Library in the extension's Options, or use the Clear all button on the page.
- Back up all your annotations to a file you keep, and restore them later.
- Removing the extension deletes everything it stored on your device.

## Children
The extension collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

## Changes
If this policy changes, the new version will be published here with a new date. If a future version ever sends data off your device (for example, to process a payment), this policy will say so before that version is released.
